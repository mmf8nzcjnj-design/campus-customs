01

Chat search updates the page with product recommendations

PASS

Feature: chat search that updates the page (Problem 7) · Page: /products

What I did

Signed in, opened the Friendly Bulldog chat, and typed: "Find me blue-colored T-shirts for my girlfriend."

What happened

  • The chat greeted me by name ("Hi Manish!") and replied that the shop has 5 blue/navy T-shirts at $32.00, highlighting three of them.
  • The Products page updated by itself with a "From your chat" section, Blue T-Shirts · 5 found, showing each match as a full product card (image, name, short description, price) marked Bulldog's pick.
  • Mini product cards with price and the sizes in stock also appear under the chat reply, and every card opens the product's detail page.
Verified against the database: exactly 5 T-shirts have navy as the garment color, all priced $32.00. The chat's count and price match.

Rationale

Shoppers describe what they want in their own words ("blue T-shirts for my girlfriend") instead of clicking through filters. The chat finds the matches and lays them out where they're easy to compare and buy.

Business value: fewer steps from intent to product page means fewer abandoned visits and more conversions. It also turns the chatbot from a help desk into a sales channel.

02

The size a shopper picked is remembered and pre-selected

PASS

Feature: usability improvement, remember my size (Problem 9) · Page: /products/district-vit-hoodie-vintage-bulldog

What I did

Added an item in size S to the bag, then opened a different product, the District Vit Hoodie Vintage Bulldog ($68.00).

What happened

  • Size S was automatically selected, with the note: "✓ We picked S, the size in your bag. Tap another to change."
  • The size row shows live stock for S ("12 in stock"), and the shopper can switch sizes with one tap.
  • The "Buy now" button is ready right away, with no extra click to choose a size.
Verified against the database: District Vit Hoodie Vintage Bulldog has 12 units in size S, matching the "12 in stock" shown. If the saved size were sold out on an item, the page would say so instead of pre-selecting it.

Rationale

A shopper wears one size, so making them pick it again on every product is a repeated, pointless click. Remembering it makes every next product one tap from "Buy now".

Business value: less friction on the second and third item raises items per order. It also supports our "fewest clicks to checkout" goal.

03

"Complete the look" recommends products based on the last item added

PASS

Feature: usability improvement, cross-sell recommendations in the bag (Problem 9) · Bag drawer

What I did

Added the Benjamin Franklin T-Shirt in size S to the bag.

What happened

  • The bag opened and confirmed: "✓ Added to your bag: Benjamin Franklin T Shirt · Size S".
  • A Complete the look panel recommended 4 products to go with a T-shirt, all in stock in size S: a hoodie ($68), a crewneck ($58), a fleece jacket ($98) and another tee ($32). Picks favor complementary layers and the same theme. For example, the Benjamin Franklin Fleece Jacket matches the Benjamin Franklin tee.
  • Each recommendation has a one-tap "+ S" button that adds it in the saved size without leaving the bag.
Verified against the database: all four recommendations have stock in size S (Hockey hoodie 15, Squash Left Chest 20, Benjamin Franklin Fleece 12, Diving tee 15), at the prices shown.

Rationale

The moment a shopper adds something is when they're most ready to buy more. Showing items that go with it, in their size and one tap away, saves them a new search.

Business value: cross-selling at add-to-bag raises average order value and items per order, which means more revenue from the same visit.

04

Chat checks the live inventory level, size by size

PASS

Feature: database tools for products, info and stock (Problem 6) · Chat widget

What I did

As a follow-up in the same chat, asked: "How many of the Boola Boola T-shirts are available?"

What happened

  • The chat answered: 61 total available: XS 12, S 12, M 15, XL 2 and XXL 20, and "It's sold out in L."
  • It understood the follow-up refers to a product from the earlier answer, and attached the product card ($32.00, sizes in stock: XS S M XL XXL).
Verified against the database (inventory table): Boola Boola T-Shirt has XS 12, S 12, M 15, L 0, XL 2, XXL 20, so 61 units in total, priced $32.00. Every number in the reply matches. The agent reads stock through its get_product_details / check_stock tools and is not allowed to guess quantities.

Rationale

People shop by their own size. A precise, honest answer per size, including "sold out in L", builds trust and stops disappointment at checkout.

Business value: accurate stock answers mean fewer failed orders, cancellations and support emails. Low counts like "XL: 2 left" also create urgency to buy now.
